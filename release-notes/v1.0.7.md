Magic Kettle 1.0.7: a white window of a Chromium-based game is caught even when the Mac is busy.

## New in this version

- **A blank Chromium window isn't let through on a busy Mac.** Games built on Chromium (NW.js, Electron, RPG Maker
  MZ) sometimes open a window that stays white under Wine; Magic Kettle looks at the window a few times and, if it
  never drew, starts the game again drawn another way. On a Mac busy enough to look fewer times than planned, the
  window could count as working without enough looks, and the white window stayed. Now Magic Kettle keeps looking
  until it has seen enough, for up to three times the usual check.

## What to watch for

- The first launch of a Chromium-based game may take a little longer to be called working when the Mac is very
  busy. Games that draw are not affected.
